# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and disclosure of another person's credentials or private data are not
accepted.

Security discussions can be adversarial without becoming personal. Critique the
claim, test, policy, or code. Use private reporting for live vulnerabilities and
give maintainers a reasonable opportunity to coordinate a fix.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
project maintainers through the repository's security/contact channels.
